# About

MIDI-GPT for Live is made by the [Metacreation Lab for Creative AI](https://metacreation.net) at Simon Fraser
University. It grew out of MMM4LIVE, started by Jeff Ens and Pia Baltazar.

## Citing

If you use MIDI-GPT in your work, please cite the paper:

```bibtex
@inproceedings{pasquier2025midigpt,
  title     = {MIDI-GPT: A Controllable Generative Model for Computer-Assisted Multitrack Music Composition},
  author    = {Pasquier, Philippe and Ens, Jeff and Fradet, Nathan and Triana, Paul and Rizzotti, Davide and Rolland, Jean-Baptiste and Safi, Maryam},
  booktitle = {Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence},
  volume    = {39},
  year      = {2025},
  url       = {https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.17011}
}
```

To cite the device itself, use the repository's
[CITATION.cff](https://github.com/Metacreation-Lab/midigpt-LIVE/blob/master/CITATION.cff) (GitHub's **Cite this repository**
button).
